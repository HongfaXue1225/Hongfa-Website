ABOUT ME

Learning, reasoning,
and building useful systems.

Hello! I’m Ben, a machine learning engineer and researcher with a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from George Washington University.

My work connects natural language processing, large language models, and the systems that bring machine learning into production. I have worked on language-model applications, recommendation systems, distributed training and inference, and end-to-end ML pipelines at Mapbox, Ford, and Netskope.

My doctoral research brought together machine learning, statistical inference, and formal reasoning to make software analysis more efficient and dependable. I was advised by Guru Venkataramani, and my dissertation explored joint statistical and formal learning for software security.

I’m interested in postdoctoral research and industry opportunities in NLP, language models, and ML systems. For research collaborations or opportunities, please get in touch.

Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my cat, drinking coffee, playing games, working out, and exploring San Francisco’s food culture.
